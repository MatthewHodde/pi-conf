# Ideation: genuinely different candidates

Read this when the Open operator fires: candidates do not exist, or the ones on the table are all one family. Do not diverge at all when evidence already favours one direction, or the user only needs an existing candidate confirmed.

## Two paths

**Quick**, when the user wants a handful: carve two or three honest territories, explore a small pool privately, return the requested count across them. No subagents, no files.

**Deep**, when the user explicitly asks for many directions, says to avoid clichés, or wants real exploration: territory isolation below.

## Carve territories before generating

One partition dimension per set, chosen by where meaning or mechanism comes from:

- problems split by causal location, affected party, or level of change
- solutions split by intervention point: change the input, the process, the feedback loop, or remove the source
- creative work splits by functional result, metaphor world, word construction, or another meaning source the task itself suggests

Tone, audience, format and boldness are modifiers, not territories. Do not mix dimensions just to reach three labels.

Write the shared exclusions once, globally. Each territory then gets one line of adjacent ground it must not enter, pointing at a sibling. Check the cut by cross-placing: a candidate that fits another territory without changing its premise means the boundary is fake, redraw. Two honest territories beat three fake ones, and an empty territory beats a padded one.

Each territory also names the tension it rides: which fact or constraint of this task changes the outcome, through what mechanism. If removing that mechanism leaves the territory reading like advice that would fit any subject, redraw it. And every candidate has to depend on that mechanism, not just carry the subject's name in its title.

A rejected cliché is a boundary, not an anchor. If removing the rejected phrase collapses most of the set, the set was still orbiting it. Redraw and regenerate.

## Isolate deep exploration

On the deep path, when territories can be explored separately and subagents exist, dispatch one per territory. Each receives only the shared goal, hard constraints, verified facts, its own territory definition and exclusions, and an output budget. It never sees another territory's candidates or any anchoring examples. The parent carves before dispatch, merges after, and ignores subagent rankings.

Without subagents, run the same briefs serially. Restate the territory and its exclusions before each, and do not carry visible candidates forward.

## Dedupe by family

Compare mechanism, promise, or central image, never surface wording. Same causal mechanism with different nouns, same metaphor world with nearby images, same promise through the same delivery: one family. A quick check is to swap the subjects of two candidates. If both still work, they share a mechanism, keep one.

Keep the strongest by, in order: fit with hard constraints, power to produce the wanted change, ease of understanding, distinctiveness. No family holds a majority of the final set unless the user asked for variants inside one.

If honest families come up short of the requested count, refill once for the thinnest territory, then report the real coverage. Never pad with rewrites.

## Names

A naming set larger than three covers at least three semantic territories and at least three construction mechanisms (dictionary words, compounds, blends, metaphor names, invented sounds). Construction variety is not semantic variety.

Show compact territory headings, one short meaning cue per candidate, shared caveats once at the end. Availability of a name (trademark, domain) stays unverified until actually checked. Never infer it from memory.

## Delivery

The requested count wins after dedup. On the quick path: no per-candidate risk paragraphs, no ranking, no unrequested extra rounds. When the user asked only to diverge, cluster and dedupe but do not rank or force a choice. When they also asked to narrow, hand the survivors to the Close operator (`judging.md`).

The deep path may include one wildcard, bolder but still inside the hard constraints, labelled as a boundary marker rather than a rank. A new round on request avoids families already used.

Persist deep ideation only when the user wants it kept: `.issues/<date-topic>/ideas.md`, or a clearer domain name such as `names.md`. Same slug and git-ignore rules as the spec/design deliverables apply.
